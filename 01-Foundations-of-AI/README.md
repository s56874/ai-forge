# Foundations of Artificial Intelligence

> **Module 1 | Microsoft Nirmaan Program**

##  Overview

This module introduces the fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL). It provides an understanding of AI history, types, real-world applications, and the importance of ethical and responsible AI.

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this module, I will be able to:

- Understand the basics of Artificial Intelligence.
- Differentiate between AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning.
- Explain the history and evolution of AI.
- Identify different types of AI.
- Explore AI applications across industries.
- Understand ethical AI principles, bias, fairness, and responsible AI.

---

- Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI)
